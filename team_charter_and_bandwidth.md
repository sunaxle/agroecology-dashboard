## 2. Operational Cadence

To minimize meeting fatigue, the team strictly adheres to the following protocol:

1. **The Asynchronous Standard:** All farm metrics, daily harvests, and trial updates must be pushed to `farm_operations.md`, `environmental_metrics.md`, or the `master_crm.csv`. The AI swarm digests these logs and surfaces key insights.
2. **Weekly Retrospective (Optional AI Assist):** A 45-minute sync at the end of the week. The team can run the `/retro` slash command to have the swarm instantly generate a summary of the week's accomplishments and bottlenecks, allowing the meeting to focus strictly on human-to-human problem solving.
3. **Task Allocation Rule:** Tasks on the `PROJECT_BOARD.md` must not be assigned solely based on urgency. They must be cross-referenced with the assigned owner's current cognitive load. If a task involves data parsing, drafting text, or scheduling, it must be delegated to an AI agent first.
