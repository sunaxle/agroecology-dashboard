# W.K. Kellogg Foundation - Project Deliverables Checklist

> [!NOTE]
> **Grant Period:** March 1, 2026 – August 31, 2027  
> **Total Funding:** $150,000  
> **Fiscal Sponsor:** The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley Foundation (UTRGV)  
> **Subawardee:** Sentli Center for Regenerative Agriculture  

This checklist outlines the key project deliverables derived from the W.K. Kellogg Foundation proposal snapshot, split by responsibilities between UTRGV (SARA) and Sentli.

## 🤝 Joint Deliverables (UTRGV & Sentli)
*Collaborative efforts spanning both the Hub of Prosperity (UTRGV) and Sentli Center Farm.*

### Training & Workforce Development
- [ ] **On-Site Trainings:** Deliver at least 10 on-site trainings on conservation agriculture and sustainable farming practices (across both sites).
- [ ] **Bilingual Recruitment:** Conduct all recruitment in both English and Spanish, providing live Spanish interpretation during workshops.
- [ ] **Participant Graduation:** Ensure a minimum of 20 participants complete the program (at least 10 hours or 5 sessions) to qualify for their certificates.

### Market Access & Infrastructure
- [ ] **Farmers' Markets Operations:** Continue operations at farmers markets at both sites to serve over 500 families annually.
- [ ] **Market Frequency:** Host at least 40 farmers' markets annually across the two sites.
- [ ] **Data Collection:** Outreach coordinator to help collect basic information on participants in civic agriculture (via real-time surveys or other modes).

### Education & Community Engagement
- [ ] **Health & Nutrition Programs:** Implement education programs through farm visits, hands-on learning, and cooking demonstrations at both farms.
- [ ] **Food Festivals:** Host 3 Farm-to-Family Food Festivals (4F) over the project period, teaching at least 100 individuals how to prepare healthy meals using local produce.
- [ ] **Community Center Assistance:** Offer technical assistance to at least 5 local community centers interested in developing farmers' markets and federal nutrition programs.
- [ ] **Market Pilot:** Ensure at least 1 location pilots a program to coordinate a farmers' market featuring local produce and entrepreneurs.
- [ ] **Regional Training:** Host at least 1 regional training session for community organizations on implementing nutrition incentive programs (WIC, Double Up Food Bucks). *(May be held on-site or at the Annual RGV Food Summit in April 2026).*

## 🏛️ UTRGV / SARA Deliverables
*Responsibilities primarily driven by the university and the Hub of Prosperity.*

### Training & Education
- [ ] **Certification:** Issue university certificates from UTRGV’s Office for Professional Education and Workforce Development to the 20+ completing participants.
- [ ] **Off-Site Training:** Deliver off-site training to community partners across the RGV via the Mobile Agroecology Learning Lab (MALL).
- [ ] **MALL Outreach (Schools/Centers):** Use the MALL to provide outreach at 4 schools or community centers in Hidalgo and Cameron Counties (distributing recipes, incentive info, market access guides).
- [ ] **MALL Outreach (Individuals):** Ensure at least 80 individuals learn where and how to purchase fresh, local produce via MALL outreach.
- [ ] **Educational Materials:** Publish materials on nutrition, agroecology, and market access on `rgvagroecology.com`.
- [ ] **Website Traffic:** Achieve at least 50 site visits annually to the educational materials.

### Incubator Farm & Market
- [ ] **Incubator Support:** Provide access to land, water, and farm tools for at least 5 small-scale farmers at the Hub of Prosperity incubator farm.
- [ ] **SNAP Exploration:** Explore the possibility of accepting SNAP at the Hub of Prosperity Farm.

---

## 🚜 Sentli Center Deliverables (Subawardee)
*Responsibilities primarily driven by Sentli Center for Regenerative Agriculture and their Growing Justice Project.*

### CSA & Wholesale Markets
- [ ] **CSA Sourcing:** Prioritize sourcing from beginning and limited-resource farmers for the CSA program.
- [ ] **CSA Producer Target:** Maintain at least 8 local producers selling to the CSA annually.
- [ ] **CSA Distribution:** Distribute healthy food options for low-income families across 5 cities along the Rio Grande Valley.
- [ ] **Wholesale Assistance:** Provide one-on-one technical assistance to at least 10 limited-resource farmers on wholesale readiness and marketing strategies.

### Market Incentives
- [ ] **SNAP Acceptance:** Ensure that the Sentli Farmers Market accepts SNAP.
- [ ] **Double Up Food Bucks:** Participate in the Double Up Food Bucks Texas program.
- [ ] **Family Utilization:** Enable at least 30 families to use the SNAP/Double Up program to purchase fresh, healthy foods.
