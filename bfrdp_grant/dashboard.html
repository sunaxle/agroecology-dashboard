USDA-NIFA Beginning Farmer and Rancher Development Program

Subtropical Agroecology Enterprises (Grant Award: $748,500)

University of Texas Rio Grande Valley · Agroecology & Resilient Food Systems Lab

Project Leadership

Lead PI / Project Director: Dr. Alexis Racelis

Narrative & Strategy: Dr. Lockman

Grants & Compliance: Aisha

Regional Scope & Target

Target Region: 8 South Texas Counties

Hub Living Lab: 5-Acre Incubator Farm

Graduate Target: 60 Certified Beginning Producers

Mentorship Cohort: 20 Peer Pairs

Key Project Deliverable Highlights

  • 36 Bilingual Workshops: Soil health, drip irrigation, IPM, and agroforestry tailored to subtropical climate.
  • Hub of Prosperity Incubator: 15 physical plots with water access, shared tools, and post-harvest cold storage.
  • Agribusiness & Financial Mentorship: 1-on-1 financial coaching via Kitchen Table Consultants and FSA loan clinics.
  • Key Regional Alliances: Active partnerships with American Farmland Trust, South Texas Grazing Land Coalition, Good Grow, and TIPA.
